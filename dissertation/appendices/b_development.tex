\section{Development protocol and source cohorts}\label{sec:sourceprotocol}
Fifteen cells use the complete initialization seeds 0--2 of configurations obtained from a 480-fit development screen and a 144-fit follow-up. Five cells use three runs from larger historical cohorts: HepPh Non-Private ($N=40$), LastFM Uniform1 ($N=200$), Facebook Uniform1 ($N=200$), HepPh PDP-PrivIM ($N=200$), and HepPh Uniform4 ($N=140$). Their original public initialization IDs are listed in Table~\ref{tab:frozendetails}. Figure~\ref{fig:parameter-sensitivity} uses 35 distinct PDP-PrivIM configurations from the 480-fit screen. It includes all 105 saved fits for the plotted settings. Each dataset's 100-update reference at $n=40$ and $M=4$ appears in all three panels.

For those five cells, the source records document a post hoc search over three-run combinations within each original configuration: a mean gate was applied before minimizing sample variance; ties used higher minimum Coverage and deterministic task identity. HepPh Non-Private did not meet the $95\%$ mean gate, and its highest three-run mean was used. Parameter configurations in the other cells were also compared using observed development Coverage. The current table consequently does not provide an unselected independent estimate of utility or stability. In particular, the three-run SDs should not replace the full-cohort SDs when characterizing their original repeat distributions.

Table~\ref{tab:subsetcohorts} summarizes all five source cohorts in full. \section{Unsuccessful exploration}
The historical no-projection PDP v1 remains a separate ablation. G3's fixed 100-step parameter average failed its five-graph screen. C3's row-sum candidate completed its fixed experiment and failed all graph-specific acceptance gates, including regressions that aggregate improvements could not cancel. Their mechanisms are not substituted for PDP-PrivIM in the current three-run table.

The development record documents mechanism exploration, retained unfavorable evidence, and unresolved stability limitations. It does not support describing every exploratory idea as validated or treating repeated development iterations as independent validation.
