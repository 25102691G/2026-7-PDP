\section{Reproducibility limits}
Public seeds, parameters, source identities, scalar outputs, and failures support protocol auditing. Fresh secret Gaussian and Bernoulli trajectories are not serialized for replay. A public seed does not reconstruct a fitted private model. Replication concerns the specified mechanism and its repeat distribution rather than identical secret paths.

All recorded low-utility results remain in the five-seed statistics. Historical diagnostics and alternative candidates are labeled separately and do not enlarge the main table's sample size. Source hashing and scalar checks establish consistency, not ideal random-source independence, machine-level privacy, or a full causal account of instability.

\section{Disclosure boundary}
Joint model release requires composition across the released models. Evaluations on private test graphs and the disclosure of selected identities may require separate privacy guarantees.
