\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
Influence maximization selects a small seed set to reach many nodes in a network. Graph neural network training can expose sensitive node information. This dissertation studies personalized node-level privacy under heterogeneous requirements and examines the substantial utility variability observed in private training. The current empirical objective is to understand instability, without requiring personalized training to outperform a strict uniform control or approach a relaxed reference.

The studied PDP v2 mechanism combines a fixed public identity domain and Registry, one-time personalized Bernoulli participation, stable overlapping slots, double clipping, and Gaussian optimization over all 2340 model coordinates. Projection constrains the final 32 feature-map weights after each update. Under explicit fixed-domain, locality, ideal-randomization, and release assumptions, the conditional analysis gives approximate epsilon bounds of 0.986940 for target 1 and 1.982093 for targets 2 and 4, with corresponding delta bounds 0.0000026894 and 0.00001. These bounds do not certify machine implementation or private evaluation.

Experiments use Email, Bitcoin, LastFM, HepPh, and Facebook with fixed prepared splits. Non-Private, strict Uniform epsilon 1, PDP v2, and relaxed Uniform epsilon 4 each have five fixed initialization seeds per graph. All 100 existing development outcomes are retained and summarized by mean and sample standard deviation. Private configurations show severe dispersion in several cells: LastFM PDP has standard deviation 32.386 percentage points, while relaxed Uniform has standard deviations 0.731 on Bitcoin and 24.121 on Facebook.

Static update analysis identifies large full-coordinate noise scales relative to the clipped signal bound. Historical controlled diagnostics show that changing Gaussian training paths can substantially alter Coverage with initialization and retention fixed. Outer sampling and evaluation randomness cannot explain all observed instability. A complete causal variance decomposition remains unavailable. The findings separate conditional privacy feasibility, observed variability, and unresolved optimization mechanisms; they establish no general stability or utility superiority claim.
