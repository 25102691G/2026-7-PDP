\section{Unsuccessful exploration}
The historical no-projection PDP v1 remains a separate ablation. G3's fixed 100-step parameter average failed its five-graph screen. C3's row-sum candidate completed its fixed experiment and failed all graph-specific acceptance gates, including regressions that aggregate improvements could not cancel. Their mechanisms are not substituted for PDP v2 in the main five-seed table.

The development record documents mechanism exploration, retained unfavorable evidence, and unresolved stability limitations. It does not support describing every exploratory idea as validated or treating repeated development iterations as independent validation.
